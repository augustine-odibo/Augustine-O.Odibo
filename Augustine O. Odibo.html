// biomaterials · hydrogels · bioelectric signaling

Augustine O. Odibo

Pharm.D · MSc Biochemical Engineering · MPhil Chemical Technology

I am a graduate research assistant in the Department of Chemical and Biological Engineering at Villanova University, working with Dr. Rodriguez on how electrical charge in biomaterials shapes stem cell behavior. My dissertation looks at charged hydrogel scaffolds as a way to direct microtissue formation and differentiation through bioelectrical signaling.

Before Villanova, I completed a Master of Philosophy in Chemical Technology at Chulalongkorn University, studying microalgal-bacterial co-cultures as living biofilters for nutrient recovery from wastewater. I hold a Doctor of Pharmacy from the University of Benin and spent several years working across pharmaceutical manufacturing, regulatory science, and analytical chemistry in Nigeria before moving into biomaterials research.

My work sits at the intersection of biomaterials design, regenerative medicine, and drug delivery — from 3D-printed hydrogel scaffolds and stem cell engineering to environmental biotechnology and pharmaceutical analysis.
